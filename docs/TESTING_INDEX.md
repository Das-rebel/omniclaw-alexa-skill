## 📊 Decision Go/No-Go Framework

After testing completes, use this framework to decide launch readiness:

### ✅ READY TO LAUNCH
- All 4 metrics meet or exceed targets
- No critical issues
- High-priority issues minimal (< 3)
- Participant feedback generally positive

**Action**: Proceed with public launch, create iteration plan for V2.1

### ⚠️ LAUNCH WITH CONDITIONS
- 3 of 4 metrics meet targets
- 1 metric close but not statistically significant
- Critical issues identified but fixable in < 1 week
- Participant feedback mixed but generally positive

**Action**: Launch after fixing critical issues, aggressive iteration plan

### ⚠️ DELAY LAUNCH
- 2 of 4 metrics meet targets
- Multiple metrics not close to targets
- Several critical issues
- Participant feedback indicates major problems

**Action**: Address issues, conduct retest with 5-10 participants

### ❌ NOT READY
- 0-1 metrics meet targets
- Fundamental usability issues
- Major technical problems
- Negative participant feedback

**Action**: Major redesign, repeat full testing process

## 🎓 Key Design Principles

This testing plan follows these principles:

1. **Validate Success Metrics**: Every test scenario ties to a metric
2. **Both Quantitative & Qualitative**: Numbers + human insights
3. **Statistical Rigor**: Confidence intervals, significance testing
4. **Actionable Outputs**: Clear recommendations, issue prioritization
5. **Quick Execution**: 3-week timeline, minimal overhead
6. **Participant-Centric**: Comfortable experience, fair compensation
